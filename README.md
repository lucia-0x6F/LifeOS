# LifeOS

LifeOS helps you turn long-term goals into actionable steps.

By connecting your vision with daily execution, LifeOS makes progress feel clear, simple, and achievable.

![LifeOS Preview](Preview.png)


## How it works
#### 1. Define your goal
Set the direction you want to move toward.

#### 2. Break it down
Transform big ambitions into manageable tasks.

#### 3. Make progress
Focus on the next step and keep moving forward.


## Design Philosophy

Less distraction. More intention.

LifeOS is built around a minimalist experience that helps you focus on what truly matters.


## Get Started

Run LifeOS directly:

- macOS/Linux: `./run.sh`
- Windows: Double-click `run.bat`

To create a desktop app:

- macOS: `./package-macos.sh`
- Windows: `package-windows.bat`


## Usage

1. Your progress is saved when you click **Save** or exit.
2. Click **Load** to restore your latest saved progress.
